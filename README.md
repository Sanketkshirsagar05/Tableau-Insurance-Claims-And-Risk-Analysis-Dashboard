# 🧾 Insurance Claims & Customer Insights Dashboard  

## 📘 Project Overview  
This Tableau dashboard provides a detailed analysis of **insurance claims**, **policyholder demographics**, and **customer insights**.  
It uncovers relationships between **claim frequency**, **household income**, **car details**, and **demographic attributes**, enabling data-driven decisions for **risk assessment** and **policy optimization**.  

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Analyze total and average **claim amounts** by demographic attributes.  
- Examine the influence of **household income**, **car type**, and **usage** on claims.  
- Identify trends in **claim frequency** to detect high-risk customer segments.  
- Support **customer segmentation** and **policy improvement** through insights.  

---

## 🧩 Dataset Overview  
**Columns Used:**  
- `age`, `age (bin)`, `age (group)`  
- `Birthdate`, `Gender`, `Marital Status`, `Parent`  
- `Car Color`, `Car Make`, `Car Model`, `Car Year`, `Car Use`  
- `Claim Amt`, `Claim Freq`, `Coverage Zone`  
- `Education`, `Household Income (hh income)`  
- `Insurance count`, `Kids Driving`, `Calculation1`, `Calculation2`  

---

## 🔑 Key KPIs  
1. **Total Claim Amount** – Total value of all claims submitted.  
2. **Claim Frequency** – Number of claims filed by policyholders.  
3. **Average Claim Amount per Policyholder** – Indicates claim severity and risk exposure.  
4. **Insurance Count (Active Policies)** – Total number of active policyholders.  

---

## 📊 Visual Insights  
- **KPI Cards:** Show overall performance metrics such as total claims and frequency.  
- **Bar Charts:** Compare claim amount by gender, car make, or income level.  
- **Pie Charts:** Visualize claim distribution by coverage zone or education level.  
- **Line Charts:** Track claim trends over time or across car year.  
- **Heat Maps:** Identify claim concentration across income and frequency categories.
